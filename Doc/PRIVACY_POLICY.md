# Privacy Policy for Cric League

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2025

Cric League ("we", "our", or "us") is built as a Privacy-First application. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Cric League does **NOT** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data to external servers or third-party advertisers. All match data, team details, player stats, and scores are saved locally on your device's internal storage using Room Database.

## 2. Permissions & Local P2P Sync
Cric League uses Google Play Services Nearby Connections API to enable local peer-to-peer (P2P) score sharing between nearby devices:
- **Bluetooth & Wi-Fi Direct Permissions** (`BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES`): Used strictly to discover and connect to nearby scoring devices.
- **Location Permission** (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`): Required by the Android OS framework to enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct scanning. Location data is never recorded, tracked, or sent anywhere.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Cric League does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
